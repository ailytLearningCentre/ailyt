AILYT IGNOU BCA / MCA PROFESSIONAL LEARNING TRACK

IGNOU provides the official academic curriculum, university study material, examinations and degree requirements. AILYT Learning Centre provides independent academic, practical, project, industrial training and career-development support alongside the student's IGNOU journey.

SUPPORT INCLUDES
- Semester-wise academic support
- Programming practice and practical/lab guidance
- Assignment guidance (not completed assignments)
- Previous-year questions and exam preparation
- Progressive live projects
- Industrial training and professional workflow exposure
- GitHub portfolio and resume development
- Interview and career preparation

BCA PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY
Year 1: Foundation, programming logic, practical work, mini projects and first GitHub exposure.
Year 2: Databases, SQL, application development, Git/GitHub, live projects and industrial training exposure.
Year 3: Advanced practical skills, capstone, industrial training, portfolio, resume, LinkedIn and interviews.

SEMESTER SUPPORT
Each semester combines academic support, practical/programming support, project or industrial training work, and career development.

IMPORTANT
AILYT does not replace IGNOU, award IGNOU degrees, guarantee marks, guarantee employment, or guarantee placement or salary. Exact batches, schedules, tools and project plans should be confirmed with AILYT during counselling.
